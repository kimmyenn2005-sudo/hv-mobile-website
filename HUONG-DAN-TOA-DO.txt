DIA CHI CHUAN (hien dang dung tren toan bo website):
    43 Le Thanh Ton, phuong Cam Thanh, Quang Ngai
    Toa do: 15.113954, 108.810143

TAI SAO TRUOC DAY GOOGLE NHAY SANG DUC PHO?
    Link cu tim theo CHU ("43 Le Thanh Ton..."). Google tu doan
    va co the tra ve chi nhanh/dia diem khac trong tinh.
    Bay gio tat ca link deu la dang GHIM TOA DO:
        https://www.google.com/maps/place/15.113954,108.810143/@15.113954,108.810143,19z
    Google mo dung diem nay, KHONG tim kiem lai -> khong the nhay di dau khac.

DOI GHIM KHI CAN (neu ghim lech vai chuc met):
    1. Mo Google Maps -> bam giu dung cua hang -> copy 2 so toa do.
    2. Mo tep  scripts/toa-do.mjs
    3. Sua 2 dong:
           export const QUANG_NGAI_LAT = "15.113954";
           export const QUANG_NGAI_LNG = "108.810143";
    4. Chay:  npm run build  roi  npm run check
    -> Toan bo footer, nut CHI DUONG va schema tren MOI trang tu cap nhat.
    KHONG can sua tay tung trang nua.

KIEM TRA SAU KHI DEPLOY (quan trong):
    Mo:  https://<ten-mien-cua-ban>/map-check.txt
    - Neu "Ban build luc" la thoi diem moi  -> may chu da chay ban moi.
    - Neu khong mo duoc hoac ngay cu       -> ban moi CHUA len,
      can deploy lai (hoac Clear cache and deploy site tren Netlify),
      roi tren dien thoai xoa cache trinh duyet / mo tab an de thu lai.
